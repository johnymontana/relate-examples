    Which documents

    Click a node or a relationship to see what it is and the sentences behind it. Faded ones, captioned "(review)", wait for your verdict: click one to accept or reject it.

    Answers between a cutoff pair, and those a rule could not settle, wait here. A verdict overrides the cutoffs and survives re-gating and re-extraction; the latest one wins.

    The ontology as a picture: each kind a node, coloured as in the graph tab, and each link a relationship between the kinds it may join. Click one to see what the model is asked and its cutoffs.

    The document (Ontology Graph Spec v1)

    Moving a cutoff re-runs the gate and the publish in Cypher over the stored candidates; it never calls the model. Every candidate scoring at least the floor (0.10) is stored.

      Download and restore

      Open the download with native Relate 
      
          
      The tar is the store's snapshot, taken right after a checkpoint: graph.json and manifest.json, a directory database that native Relate 1.2.0 adopts on its first writable open (a read-only open or relate clone needs that open first). Going the other way, relate clone --to-snapshot-format json kg.rl dir, then python3 -I lib/opfsfmt.py to-tar dir kg.tar, restores here. dump.json is the engine-independent format of lib/replay.
