## the floor invariant: cutoffs never need the model (D7)

query on the store) must equal what gliner-rs returns when run at threshold t on the same sentence:
entity spans with their labels, and relation edges with their spans. A difference is a row below.

ontology. Thresholds: 0.20, 0.30, 0.40, 0.50, 0.60, 0.70, 0.80, 0.90.

The differences are gliner-rs decoding, not storage: with the flat overlap policy an entity span picked at
the floor can give way to a longer overlapping one once the lower-scoring candidates are gone, and a
relation's head or tail span is picked per threshold (under every overlap policy). A cutoff in the store
filters what the floor found and never re-decodes, so these spans differ from a fresh call at that threshold.
