purpose (data/samples/ccg/distractors.json).
As edgextract's eval: entities are (NAME, TYPE), relations (SOURCE, TARGET, TYPE), names canonical (names.js);
micro-averaged over the documents. Predictions are the kept band; review and drop count as not predicted.

of a canonical name): the text writes "Dr. Imani Okafor", the gold "Imani Okafor".
Kinds and links are compared as written: create-context-graph's own labels (Person, Account) and types (WORKS_FOR).
The samples' gold holds only relations their domain's ontology can hold: 303 of 303 legal for their (source kind, target kind) pair.
