## the console's bookkeeping filter (web/console.js consoleResult: "Hide the example's bookkeeping", off by default)
The extraction's own records are bookkeeping; the ontology layer and the published entities are content. A row is left
out when a top-level column is a bookkeeping node or relationship, or a path through one; inside lists and maps those
values are dropped and the row stays. NVL draws only what the shown rows hold, capped after the filter (1,000 nodes,
3,000 relationships). _IN and _OF are the ontology's between two ontology-layer nodes and bookkeeping otherwise; the
console worker sends the store's ontology node ids (ONTOLOGY_NODE_IDS) whenever the rows hold one.
  bookkeeping labels: _Document, _Section, _Sentence, _Inference, _InferencePart, _Mention, _Claim, _Run, _Verdict, _Gold
  content labels: _Ontology, _Kind, _LinkType, _Alias, _Entity (and every ontology label)
  bookkeeping types: _HEAD, _TAIL, _EVIDENCE, _READ, _ON, _INFERRED; content: _DOMAIN, _RANGE (and every ontology type); by their ends: _IN, _OF
