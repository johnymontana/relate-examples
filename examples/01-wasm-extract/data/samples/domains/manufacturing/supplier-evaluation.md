# Supplier evaluation: Brackenfold Diecasting Ltd, second half of 2026

**Prepared by:** Tom Ashworth, Supplier Quality, Calder Bridge Plant · **For:** Purchasing and the Plant Quality Review · **Date:** 27 November 2026 · **Supplier code:** S10442

## The supplier

Brackenfold Diecasting Ltd is a high-pressure aluminium die-caster in Barnsley with eleven cells from 400 to 1,600 tonnes. Brackenfold Diecasting Ltd supplies 30418-02, the HG40 pump body, and 30419-01, the HG40 rear cover, both as single-source castings. Brackenfold was approved in 2021 and passed our process audit in April 2026 with no major findings.

## Nonconformances this year

| NCR | Raised | Supplier lot | Part | Defect | Disposition |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| NCR-2026-0233 | 12 May 2026 | L26-0611 | 30419-01 | flash on the mounting face | reworked at the supplier |
| NCR-2026-0412 | 14 Oct 2026 | L26-0917 | 30418-02 | gas porosity at the relief-valve boss | returned to the supplier |

NCR-2026-0233 was attributed to Brackenfold Diecasting Ltd in May and closed in June, after the trim tool was reground. NCR-2026-0412 was attributed to Brackenfold as well, and it is the more serious of the two: it was reported by Fenmoor Tractors Ltd, so the defect reached a customer before we found it. L26-0917 was delivered by Brackenfold on 22 September and was cast on DC-104 while the machine's vacuum valve was sticking.

## Corrective actions

CA-1193, owned by Tom Ashworth, replaced the vacuum valve on DC-104 and interlocked the cell's vacuum alarm so that the cell stops when the vacuum is lost. It was implemented on 30 October and verified during our visit on 24 November. L26-1030, cast while the valve was being replaced, was made on DC-102 and not on DC-104. Since then, three lots of 30418-02 have passed X-ray at goods-in with no pore larger than 0.2 mm.

CA-1195, the X-ray check of five castings from every lot, stays open until six lots in a row have passed. CA-1195 will be handed to Rachel Okonkwo in January, when supplier quality moves into the plant quality team.

## Rating and decision

Brackenfold scores 2.9 out of 5 for the half year (quality 2, delivery 3, response 5), against 4.1 in the first half. Its 8D response was prompt and thorough, but a sticking valve and a bypassed alarm on a critical cell are a failure of the supplier's own process control.

Brackenfold Diecasting Ltd moves from approved to probationary for six months from 1 December 2026, on these conditions:

1. Rejected parts per million at or below 500 in every month of the probation.
2. Vacuum-level records for every shot of 30418-02 and 30419-01 sent with each delivery.
3. A follow-up process audit of DC-104 and DC-102 in March 2027.

If Brackenfold meets the conditions by June 2027, it would also supply 30463-01, the HG63 pump body, from the second half of 2027.
