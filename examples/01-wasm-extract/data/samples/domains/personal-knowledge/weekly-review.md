# Weekly review: 2026-W47

**Vault:** Commonplace · **Period:** Monday 16 to Sunday 22 November 2026 · **Written:** Sunday 22 November 2026 · **Reference:** reviews/2026-W47

## The week in brief

A good week for the client work and a slow one for everything else. The Harbourside Wayfinding Study finally left the planning stage, the floor of the Garden Studio Build went down on Saturday, and issue 14 of the Slow Reading Newsletter went out on Thursday, a day late. I wrote five permanent notes, finished one book and kept the journal on five of seven days, which is better than October.

## Projects

### Harbourside Wayfinding Study

The Harbourside Wayfinding Study was commissioned by the Harbourside Community Trust in October, and this was its first week of fieldwork. Owen Pritchard works at the Harbourside Community Trust as its volunteer coordinator and is my client contact there. Leila Haddad, an illustrator at Lantern & Loom, collaborates on the Harbourside study: she sketches sign concepts while I run the walking interviews. We walked two of the six routes, on Wednesday and Friday, both in the rain. The Harbourside Wayfinding Study has a budget of £9,500 and its final report is due on 18 December 2026.

If the board agrees to a second phase in December, Owen Pritchard would collaborate on the Harbourside study as a co-researcher.

### Garden Studio Build

The floor is in. Rob Ellery of Ellery & Daughters Joinery collaborates on the Garden Studio Build: he cut the floor cassettes in his Keynsham workshop, and we set them on the piers on Saturday. The wall panels follow once the breather membrane arrives, probably the week after next. The Garden Studio Build has a budget of £16,000, and £14,200 of it is spent. It should be weathertight by 15 January 2027 if the membrane arrives on time.

### Slow Reading Newsletter

Issue 14 went to 412 subscribers on Thursday. Saoirse Nolan collaborates on the Slow Reading Newsletter as its proofreader, and caught two wrong dates in this issue before it went out. Danny Achebe might write for the Slow Reading Newsletter in the spring, but so far we have only talked about it over coffee.

## Meetings

- On Tuesday 17 November 2026, Owen Pritchard and Leila Haddad attended the Wayfinding Kickoff in the back office on Hotwell Road, which went through the Harbourside study's interview plan and settled the six routes.
- The November Mentoring Call with Hannah Okafor on Wednesday 18 November 2026 was mostly about the Slow Reading Newsletter: whether to move it to fortnightly, and whether it should ever earn money. Her answer to both was not yet.
- The Studio Site Visit on Saturday 21 November 2026 covered the Garden Studio Build's floor and the order of the wall panels. Rob Ellery arrived at eight with the cassettes and a borrowed laser level, and the floor was down by three.
