# Escalation: CS-771204, a stand mixer delivered cracked twice

**From:** Marisol Fenn, Customer Relations Team Leader · **To:** Oliver Brannigan, Head of Customer Experience, Hollins & Garth · **Date:** 24 November 2026 · **Reference:** ESC-2026-0117

## Summary

Delphine Arkwright raised CS-771204 on 17 November 2026, after the second Avonmere Atelier stand mixer we sent her arrived, like the first, with its glass bowl cracked at the base. The case meets two of our escalation criteria: a repeat failure on one order, and a public one-star review. A second customer returned the same mixer for the same fault earlier this month, from a different fulfilment centre. I recommend a full refund for Ms Arkwright, a quality hold on the mixer at both fulfilment centres, and a supplier claim against Avonmere.

Ms Arkwright, of Kettering, has shopped with us since 2021 and is in the VIP segment, with eleven orders and no previous returns.
