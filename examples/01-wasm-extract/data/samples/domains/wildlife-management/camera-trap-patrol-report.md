# Camera-trap and patrol report: Lomunyak Conservancy, July to September 2026

**From:** Grace Wanjiru, Monitoring Officer · **To:** Conservancy Manager and Board of Trustees · **Date:** 9 October 2026 · **Reference:** LMC-MON-2026-Q3

## Summary

The third quarter was good for the animals we watch most closely and bad for snares. The camera-trap grid recorded the first calf of a known rhino cow, the largest herd of Grevy's zebra on the northern grassland since 2023 and a collared wild dog pack whose collar has failed. Patrols lifted fourteen wire snares on the river and counted 320 cattle inside the grazing-free zone across the boundary. No rhino was lost, and no carcass of any protected species was found.

## The conservancy and the team

Lomunyak Conservancy is 184 km² of private land in Laikipia County, with a fenced rhino sanctuary of 62 km² at its heart. Lomunyak Conservancy contains riverine woodland along the Ewaso Ng'iro, whistling-thorn bushland on the black-cotton plateau and open grassland on the northern ridges. Ntaletiti Community Conservancy, our neighbour across the river, also contains open grassland, which Samburu herders graze in the dry season under a plan agreed with us.

Daniel Lekalau, the sergeant in charge of the rapid response unit, is stationed at Lomunyak Conservancy, and so are Ranger Mary Nkatha, who runs the camera-trap grid with me, and Ranger Joseph Ekiru, the unit's tracker. Peter Lempaa, head of the community scouts, is stationed at Ntaletiti Community Conservancy and works with our unit on the shared boundary.

## The camera-trap grid

The grid has 18 camera traps on a 2 km lattice. Sixteen ran for the whole quarter, for 1,402 trap-nights and 611 independent detections of 24 mammal species; two were out of action for part of August after their posts were pushed over. Four stations matter to this report. CT-03, CT-07 and CT-14 are deployed in Lomunyak Conservancy, while CT-11 is the only camera trap of the grid deployed in Ntaletiti Community Conservancy, on the cattle track that crosses the boundary.

**Rhino.** Nalangu is a black rhino cow of the sanctuary's founder stock, now twelve years old. SG-4410 identified Nalangu by the two notches in her left ear, with a calf of about four months at heel: the first record of the calf, which has not yet been named. She has lived in Lomunyak Conservancy all her life. Saruni, the sanctuary's breeding bull, is a black rhino too. Saruni lives in Lomunyak Conservancy and holds the river section of the sanctuary. By day the black rhino inhabits whistling-thorn bushland, and both animals come down to the river to drink after dark.

**Lion.** SG-4417 identified Lekuton, the collared lion who holds the river pride, walking the bushland track below CT-14 at 02:14. Lekuton crossed into Ntaletiti Community Conservancy three times in August, each time at night. He lives in Lomunyak Conservancy, but the cattle bomas on the far bank draw him across the river.

**Grevy's zebra and leopard.** SG-4431 recorded Grevy's zebra at the CT-11 waterhole: a herd of eleven with two foals. Grevy's zebra inhabits open grassland, and a herd that size on the Ntaletiti side suggests the grazing plan is working. In this landscape the leopard inhabits riverine woodland almost exclusively, and the same female has walked past CT-03 for three quarters running.

## Patrols

Rangers and scouts ran 71 patrols this quarter, 54 on foot and 17 by vehicle, over 2,380 km. The three below bear on the findings of this report.

| Patrol | Date | Mode | Led by | Area covered | Result |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PTL-0912 | 3 Aug 2026 | foot | Daniel Lekalau | Lomunyak Conservancy | 14 wire snares lifted |
| PTL-0918 | 21 Aug 2026 | vehicle | Peter Lempaa | Ntaletiti Community Conservancy | 320 cattle in the grazing-free zone |
| PTL-0921 | 6 Sep 2026 | foot | Joseph Ekiru | Lomunyak Conservancy | pack sighted, no snares |

PTL-0912 found evidence of snaring along the dry river bed below CT-14: fourteen wire snares set on the game trails, two of them fresh. In Laikipia, snaring threatens Grevy's zebra more than any other cause, and Sergeant Lekalau's team photographed each site before lifting the snares.

PTL-0918 recorded livestock incursion on the Ntaletiti side, with 320 cattle grazing inside the zone the plan keeps free for wildlife until November. On this ground livestock incursion threatens Grevy's zebra by taking the grass the herds need in the late dry season.

PTL-0921, a foot patrol led by Ranger Joseph Ekiru, covered the northern ridges of Lomunyak Conservancy and logged SG-4423, a sighting of Naserian and nine other adults of her pack at a waterhole. Mary Nkatha was with the patrol. She reported the sighting as SG-4423 and photographed the collar, which stopped transmitting on 28 August. Naserian, the collared alpha female of the Ntaletiti pack, is an African wild dog. Naserian lives in Ntaletiti Community Conservancy, and the pack denned there in June.

Both snaring and retaliatory poisoning threaten African wild dog: a snared dog cannot keep up with the pack, and a poisoned carcass left out for predators can kill a whole pack. After the August cattle losses, retaliatory poisoning threatens lion on both sides of the river, and Lekuton is the most exposed animal we know.

PTL-0921 found no evidence of rhino poaching on the sanctuary fence line, and no incursion has been recorded inside the sanctuary since March 2025. Even so, rhino poaching remains the gravest threat to black rhino in Laikipia, and the night patrols on the fence continue.

- If the board approves the exchange posting, Ranger Mary Nkatha would be stationed at Ntaletiti Community Conservancy from January to train its scouts in camera-trap work.

## Recommendations

1. Run a weekly foot patrol of the river bed below CT-14 until the short rains.
2. Ask the Kenya Wildlife Service veterinary unit to replace Naserian's collar before the pack moves off the den.
3. Share the PTL-0918 cattle count with the Ntaletiti grazing committee, and agree a fine schedule for repeat incursions.
4. Name Nalangu's calf and add it to the sanctuary register once it is a year old.
