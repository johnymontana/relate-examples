# Boundary survey report: Parcel 0412-180-007, Willow Bend Open Space

**From:** Marisol Ortega, PLS 38214, Deputy County Surveyor, Tamarack County Surveyor's Office · **To:** Tamarack County Parks and Open Space; Tamarack County GIS Division · **Date:** October 14, 2026 · **Survey:** TCS-2026-014

## Summary

Survey TCS-2026-014 is a boundary survey that covers Parcel 0412-180-007, the Willow Bend Open Space, a tract of 18.62 acres where Parks and Open Space plans a trailhead and parking area for 2027. The record boundary holds on the ground: we recovered two of the three corners monumented in 1998, replaced the third, set the fourth for the first time, and found every record line within 0.15 feet of the monuments. The county's GIS map does not hold. The Cadastral Fabric layer draws the parcel about 11 feet east of its surveyed position, and the Brandt barn, which that layer shows wholly on the neighboring parcel, encroaches on Parcel 0412-180-007 by 2.3 feet.

## Record research

Parcel 0412-180-007 is owned by Tamarack County under a deed of 1987 (Reception No. 87021455). Parcel 0412-180-006, owned by the Brandt Family Trust, adjoins it on the north. On the east, Parcel 0412-180-011, a tract of 6.40 acres, adjoins Parcel 0412-180-007 along the Fenn Ditch. It was annexed to Alder Springs in 2004, and its owner of record is Lucia Fenn.

The only earlier survey of the parcel is LSP-1998-0412, the 1998 Haskins survey, a boundary survey conducted by Raymond Haskins, PLS 17488, then in private practice. LSP-1998-0412 covers Parcel 0412-180-007 and was filed with the Tamarack County Clerk and Recorder on June 3, 1998. Haskins held the north line of Section 14 from its original corners and capped three corners of the parcel.

## Field work and control

Marisol Ortega and Devin Achterberg, a party chief with the Tamarack County Surveyor's Office, conducted the field work for TCS-2026-014 between September 8 and September 19, 2026. TCS-2026-014 retraced LSP-1998-0412 corner by corner, using GNSS real-time kinematic observations from control point CP-101 and a total station for the corners under tree cover.

Survey TCS-2026-014 is referenced to NAD 83(2011) for horizontal positions and to NAVD 88 for elevations, both maintained by the National Geodetic Survey. Bearings are grid bearings, and distances are ground distances in US survey feet at a combined scale factor of 0.999742. TCS-2026-014 is expected to be re-referenced to NATRF2022 once the National Geodetic Survey releases the modernized reference frames.

TCS-2026-014 recovered MON-1 and MON-2, which mark the northwest and northeast corners of Parcel 0412-180-007, and found both within 0.15 feet of their record positions. TCS-2026-014 did not recover MON-3: a search with a magnetic locator over a 10-foot radius turned up only a broken fence post. MON-4 now marks the southeast corner of Parcel 0412-180-007, at the record position of MON-3 computed from MON-1 and MON-2. MON-5, set at the southwest corner of Parcel 0412-180-007, is the first monument ever placed there; the 1998 plat left that corner unmonumented in the creek bottom.

## Boundary lines

Parcel 0412-180-007 is bounded on the north by the north line of Section 14, on the east by the Alder Springs city limit, and on the south by the County Road 38E right-of-way line. The Alder Springs city limit follows the Fenn Ditch along the whole east line, as the 2004 annexation map describes it. The west line runs from MON-5 to MON-1 and matches its record bearing to within 12 seconds.

Mill Creek crosses the west third of the parcel in a channel it cut during the September 2013 flood. Mill Creek is mapped in the Hydrography layer along its old channel, about 40 feet east of where it runs today.

## Encroachments

The Brandt barn crosses the north line for 38 feet and stands 2.3 feet inside the parcel at its southeast corner. The barn was already on the line when the trust bought Parcel 0412-180-006 in 1979, and no fence or written agreement marks a different line. The Fenn Ditch may encroach on Parcel 0412-180-007 where its west bank has slumped near MON-2, but the ditch company's easement has not been found in the record.

## Comparison with the county GIS

Parcel 0412-180-007 is mapped in the Cadastral Fabric layer, which the Tamarack County GIS Division maintains along with the Hydrography layer. The Cadastral Fabric layer is referenced to NAD 83(HARN) and was digitized in 1994 from the assessor's tax maps, not from surveys. Measured against the monuments, the layer's polygon for the parcel lies 10.8 to 11.4 feet east and 2.1 feet south of the surveyed lines. The shift from NAD 83(HARN) to NAD 83(2011) accounts for less than 0.2 feet of that offset; the rest is digitizing error.

The Open Space Atlas 2027, the GIS Division's map project for the county's open space, includes the Cadastral Fabric layer and the Hydrography layer, so the offset shows on every atlas sheet that covers the Willow Bend Open Space. If the GIS Division adjusts the parcel to this survey, the Open Space Atlas 2027 would also include the Survey Control layer, so that readers can see which corners rest on surveyed monuments.

An earlier draft of this report said that LSP-1998-0412 is referenced to NAD 83(HARN); that was wrong, since the 1998 survey used assumed bearings and no datum at all.

## Recommendations

1. Accept the boundary of Parcel 0412-180-007 as monumented by TCS-2026-014.
2. Adjust the parcel in the Cadastral Fabric layer to the surveyed corners, and Mill Creek in the Hydrography layer to its 2026 channel, before the atlas goes to review.
3. Offer the Brandt Family Trust a boundary line agreement or a license for the barn before trailhead construction starts, and keep the parking area 10 feet clear of the north line until then.
4. TCS-2026-014 will be filed with the Tamarack County Clerk and Recorder as a land survey plat on or about November 2, 2026, once Parks and Open Space accepts this report.
