# Atlantic Shore Open 2026: Kessler wins by two on the Sandhill Course

**From:** Meridian Tour media office · **To:** accredited media and broadcast partners · **Date:** Sunday 21 June 2026 · **Release:** MT-2026-118

## Summary

Mara Kessler won the Atlantic Shore Open on Sunday, closing with a 67 in the final round on the Sandhill Course for an 18-under-par total of 270 and a two-stroke victory. Tamsin Rourke finished second on 16 under, Ingrid Solvang third on 15 under and Hana Okabe, who led at halfway, fourth on 12 under. Kessler birdied the 16th and the 18th on the way in, while Rourke, one behind on the 17th tee, bogeyed the 17th after her tee shot found the deep bunker short and left of the green. The title is Kessler's third on the Meridian Tour and her first in Ireland, and the Atlantic Shore Open carried a purse of €2.5 million, of which the winner's cheque is worth €375,000.

## The venue and the format

Ardmorran Golf Club, on the Donegal coast near Dunfanaghy, owns two links courses: the Sandhill Course, laid out in 1931, and the Strand Course, which opened in 2004. Ardmorran Golf Club has hosted the Atlantic Shore Open every year since the first edition in 2019, and the event has been sanctioned by the Meridian Tour from the start.

The Atlantic Shore Open is played over the Sandhill Course and the Strand Course. The field is split between the two for the first round and the second round, so that every player has one round on each, and the third round and the final round are played on the Sandhill Course alone. Both courses play to a par of 72: the Sandhill Course measures 6,742 yards and the Strand Course 6,511 yards.

## The closing stretch

The Sandhill Course finishes with a testing run of holes. The 16th, a par 4 of 418 yards into the prevailing wind, gave up only nine birdies all week. The 17th on the Sandhill Course, a par 3 of 171 yards played over a dune to a shelf green, was the hardest hole of the championship at an average of 3.38. The 18th, the Sandhill Course's only par 5, measures 488 yards, and most of the leaders could reach it in two.

Kessler's 67 took her to 13 under par and into a lead she did not give up.

## The winner

Kessler, a member of the Meridian Tour since 2021, has now won three times in fourteen months. Declan Furey, who has caddied for Mara Kessler since 2023, talked her out of a driver on the 18th tee and into a 3-wood that left 224 yards to the green. Her second shot finished 15 feet from the hole, and two putts gave her the birdie. Tidewell Watches, which has sponsored Kessler since her rookie season, said on Sunday evening that it would extend her contract to the end of 2029.

## The challengers

Tamsin Rourke, who is sponsored by Aldmark, drew level with Kessler after a birdie at the 14th. Rab McAteer, Rourke's caddie for six seasons, said the bunker at the 17th was the only bad break of her week. Rourke, Okabe and Solvang are all members of the Meridian Tour, and Rourke has now finished second three times this season.

Ingrid Solvang, playing in the Atlantic Shore Open for the first time, finished third despite a two-stroke penalty under Rule 14.7 for playing from a wrong place at the 11th in the second round. Okabe recovered from Saturday's 72 with a 70 on Sunday to take fourth place. Her caddie, Ollie Haldane, carried for her all week on a strapped knee.

## The leading amateur

Niamh Corcoran, a 19-year-old who represents Ardmorran Golf Club, was the leading amateur on five under par and the only amateur to make the cut in the Atlantic Shore Open. Corcoran is expected to join the Meridian Tour in September, when she turns professional. Aldmark, which already sponsors Rourke, has offered to sponsor Corcoran as well if she does.
