# Guest incident report: water ingress on the west stack

**From:** Rhona Gilchrist, Night Duty Manager · **To:** Callum Reid, General Manager, Halden Quay Hotel · **Date:** 16 November 2026 · **Reference:** INC-2026-0418

## Summary

At about 02:40 on Saturday 14 November 2026, a braided supply hose under the basin in Room 714 split, and water came down through the ceilings of the two rooms below. INC-2026-0418 started in Room 714, a vacant deluxe room that had been checked out the previous afternoon. INC-2026-0418 affected two guests, Tomasz Wierzbicki in Room 614 and Odette Fairlie in Room 514. Nobody was hurt, and I have rated INC-2026-0418 moderate. Both guests were out of their rooms within an hour, both have since complained, and Room 514 stays out of order until its ceiling and bathroom light are replaced.

The Halden Quay Hotel is a 164-room upscale hotel on the Shore in Leith; it offers the Tidewater Spa, a Club Lounge on the tenth floor and breakfast in the brasserie. Room 714, Room 614 and Room 514 sit one above the other on the west stack of the Halden Quay Hotel and share a single riser. 2. Trace any ceiling leak to the room above before the incident is closed, and reopen INC-2026-0409 under that rule.

4. Add a check of wardrobes and drawers to the housekeeping checklist for any room a guest leaves in an emergency.
5. Close INC-2026-0418 once both complaints are resolved and Room 514 is back in service.
