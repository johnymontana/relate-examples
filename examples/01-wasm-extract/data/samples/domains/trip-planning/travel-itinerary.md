Margaret Ashworth, the lead traveller, and David Ashworth travel on ITN-4417 from start to finish, to mark their fortieth wedding anniversary. ## Day by day

### Friday 14 May: arrival in Lisbon

FW1832 arrives at Lisbon's Humberto Delgado Airport at 10:05, and a driver will be waiting in the arrivals hall with your name on a board. Humberto Delgado Airport serves both Lisbon and Sintra, and Casa Verbena is about twenty-five minutes away by car. Casa Verbena is a 20-room townhouse two streets above the river; your room is ready from 14:00, and the afternoon is yours.

### Saturday 15 May: Lisbon

The Petiscos Food Walk is a three-hour walk through the old quarters of Lisbon, with seven small plates and a glass of ginjinha on the way. It is run by Sete Colinas Walks, whose guide meets you at the hotel door at 10:30. David Ashworth booked it as a surprise, so BKG-50314 is in his name.

For dinner, BKG-50313 holds a table for two at Taberna Fio de Azeite at 20:00, by the window. Taberna Fio de Azeite, a family-run restaurant, is a five-minute walk from Casa Verbena, and the owner knows about the anniversary.

### Sunday 16 May: Sintra

The Palaces and Gardens Day Tour is run by Serra Clara Tours, whose driver collects you from Casa Verbena at 08:30. BKG-50315 holds the two places for £260 in all. The tour takes place in Sintra, in the wooded hills west of Lisbon, and includes timed entry to two palaces and a long lunch in the old town. Wear shoes with a good grip: the paths are steep and often damp.

### Monday 17 May: Lisbon at leisure

A free day. The concierge can arrange a tram pass or a table for lunch, and we have left a list of our favourite museums in your travel wallet.

### Tuesday 18 May: Lisbon to Porto

BKG-50316 reserves two first-class seats on the 10:39 train to Porto, which arrives at 13:30. FW1854 departs from London Heathrow at 09:40 and lands at Francisco Sá Carneiro Airport at 12:25, so Fiona Kerr should reach Hotel Vinhal before you do.

Hotel Vinhal is a 1920s merchant's house near the cathedral. Adega do Postigo, a wine bar around the corner from Hotel Vinhal, does not take bookings, so go before 19:30 for its grilled octopus.

### Wednesday 19 May: Porto

The Twilight Port Tasting takes place in Vila Nova de Gaia, across the river from Porto, and is run by Duas Margens Tours. Fiona Kerr asked to treat you both, so BKG-50318 is in her name; the tasting starts at 18:00 in a cellar that has stored port since 1890.

### Thursday 20 May: Porto to the Douro Valley

A driver from Socalco Experiences collects the three of you from Hotel Vinhal at 10:00 for the two-hour drive east. Quinta do Vale Sereno, a working wine estate in the Douro Valley, has 11 rooms above its terraces and a pool that looks down on the river. For dinner away from the estate, Casa de Pasto Xisto, a small restaurant in the village, is ten minutes' walk down the hill.

### Friday 21 May: Douro Valley

The Rabelo Boat Trip, run by Socalco Experiences, takes place in the Douro Valley: three hours upriver from the quinta's jetty in a traditional rabelo boat, with lunch on board and a stop at a terraced vineyard. BKG-50321 reserves three places on the Rabelo Boat Trip, so Fiona Kerr comes too.

### Saturday 22 May: Douro Valley at leisure

A free day at the quinta, which pours its own wines for guests at 17:00 with no need to book.

### Sunday 23 May: home

Francisco Sá Carneiro Airport serves the Douro Valley as well as Porto, but the drive takes two hours, so Socalco Experiences will collect you from Quinta do Vale Sereno at 13:30. FW1879 departs from Francisco Sá Carneiro Airport at 17:50, and Fiona Kerr flies home with you on the same flight.
