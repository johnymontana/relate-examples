# Summer 2026 season performance report: Algarve and Mallorca

**From:** Marta Quintela, Director of Revenue · **To:** Executive Committee, Tamarel Leisure Group · **Date:** 9 November 2026 · **Reference:** SPR-S26-02

## Summary

Summer 2026 ran from 1 April to 31 October and was the best season since Tamarel Leisure Group took over its two resorts in 2021. Occupancy across the portfolio was 86%, four points up on Summer 2025, and revenue per available room rose 9% to €213. Ponta Dourada Resort carried the growth, on the back of family packages sold through tour operators. Cala Serena Resort held its rate but lost ground in July, when a heat warning closed the mountain trails for eleven days, and its guest score slipped for the first time in three seasons.

## The portfolio

Tamarel Leisure Group operates Ponta Dourada Resort and Cala Serena Resort. Ponta Dourada Resort is in the Algarve, on the cliffs of the western coast, with 214 rooms and villas. Cala Serena Resort, a 168-room resort and spa, stands on the north-east coast of Mallorca.

## Packages and channels

Three packages carried most of the season's revenue.

| Package | Resort | Sold by | Nights | Lead-in price per person |
|:---|:---|:---|---:|---:|
| Family Sun Week | Ponta Dourada Resort | Brightwater Holidays | 7 | €890 |
| Serena Spa Escape | Cala Serena Resort | direct only | 3 | €640 |
| Tramuntana Active Week | Cala Serena Resort | Sonnwend Reisen | 7 | €980 |

The Family Sun Week is offered at Ponta Dourada Resort and is sold by Brightwater Holidays, a British tour operator that holds an allocation of 40 family rooms. It includes the Polvo Club for children aged four to twelve and one Benagil Caves Kayak Tour per family.

The Serena Spa Escape, a three-night break at Cala Serena Resort, is sold only direct, through our own website and reservations team, and includes two sessions of the Thalasso Ritual. Brightwater Holidays does not sell the Serena Spa Escape, which we keep for direct bookings.

Sonnwend Reisen, a German tour operator in its first season with us, sells the Tramuntana Active Week. The Tramuntana Active Week is offered at Cala Serena Resort and includes the Tramuntana Hiking Day on four of its seven days. If the trial goes well, the Family Sun Week would also be sold by Sonnwend Reisen from next summer.

The Family Sun Week, the Serena Spa Escape and the Tramuntana Active Week were all available in Summer 2026.

The Sunset Catamaran Cruise is run by Barlavento Charters and ran in Summer 2026 from 1 May to 15 October; it sold out on 61 evenings, and we have asked for a second boat in August. Rocha Azul Kayaks runs the Benagil Caves Kayak Tour, which lost nine mornings to sea swell in September but still reached 84%. Ponta Dourada also offers the Polvo Club, run by the resort's own team, which took 11,950 child-days.

The Tramuntana Hiking Day is run by Serra Nord Guides and had a difficult summer. A heat warning closed the trails from 12 to 22 July, and utilisation fell to 63%. The Tramuntana Hiking Day will also run in Winter 2026/27, when cooler weather should suit it better. The Thalasso Ritual, Cala Serena's signature treatment, ran at 88% of capacity.

An earlier draft of this report said that the Benagil Caves Kayak Tour was run by Barlavento Charters; that was wrong.

## Winter 2026/27

Winter 2026/27 is the first winter in which Cala Serena Resort stays open. The Serena Spa Escape is on sale for it, and forward bookings stand at 41% of target. The winter lead-in price for the Serena Spa Escape is expected to settle at about €590 per person once the spa's opening hours are fixed. Ponta Dourada Resort closes for its refurbishment from 2 November to 20 March.
