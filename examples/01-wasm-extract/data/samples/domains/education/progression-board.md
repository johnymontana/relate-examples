## 2. Remit

The Board met under Section 7 of the Undergraduate Assessment Regulations to confirm Stage 2 marks for 2025/26 and to decide each student's progression to Stage 3. Marks are provisional until the Board confirms them, and the pass mark for every assessment is 40%.

## 3. Programmes and modules

The BSc (Hons) Biomedical Science and the BSc (Hons) Biochemistry are offered by the School of Biosciences. BIO2041, BIO2110 and BIO2203 are core modules of the BSc (Hons) Biomedical Science. The BSc (Hons) Biochemistry shares BIO2041 and BIO2110 and adds BCH2310 Metabolic Biochemistry, which Dr Owain Pritchard leads. BIO2041 and BCH2310 are taught in Semester 1, BIO2203 in Semester 2, and BIO2110 runs through Semester 1 and Semester 2.

## 4. External examiner's report

Dr Rosalind Gethin confirmed that marking in all four modules was consistent and that standards matched those of comparable programmes elsewhere. She praised the written feedback on the BIO2110 data-analysis project. She asked the Board to look again at the BIO2041 practical report, whose pass rate fell from 89% in 2024/25 to 71% this year.

## 5. BIO2041 Cell Biology

Dr Hannah Leclerc, who teaches BIO2041, presented the module report. BIO2041 carries 20 credits and is assessed by the BIO2041 examination (60%) and the BIO2041 practical report (40%). LO1 and LO2 are assessed by the BIO2041 examination. The BIO2041 practical report is the only assessment of LO4, which asks students to design, run and interpret a controlled cell-culture experiment. Under regulation 7.4 a module cannot be passed by compensation while one of its learning outcomes is unmet, so a fail in the practical report has to be redeemed by reassessment.

The fall in the pass rate followed a change of imaging protocol in Semester 1. The Board agreed that the earlier protocol will be restored for 2026/27 and that the marking criteria for the report will be published with the brief. From 2026/27 the BIO2041 practical report is expected to carry 50% of the module mark.

## 7. Academic integrity

The Academic Integrity Officer referred one case. The deadline for the BIO2110 data-analysis project was 15 April 2026. SUB-48213, Jordan Pike's submission for the BIO2110 data-analysis project, was handed in on 14 April 2026 and marked by Dr Ines Carvalho, who found that its R code closely matched another student's. That submission, SUB-48190, was made by Freya Lindqvist for the same project and marked by Liam Osei. Both students told the panel that they had written the analysis together in a study group. The panel found poor academic practice rather than collusion: Jordan Pike's project mark was capped at 40%, and Freya Lindqvist received a written warning.
