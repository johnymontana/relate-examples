## Incidents

Both incidents stay open in the HSE system until their close-out reviews.

### IR-26-0417, spill of oil-based mud

IR-26-0417, logged at 07:05, occurred at the Hollis Ranch 31-18 2H well. IR-26-0417 involved mud pump 2: the 2-inch hammer union on its discharge line washed out and sprayed 10.2 ppg oil-based mud across the pad. The driller stopped the pumps within a minute; 7 bbl stayed on the pad liner and about 2 bbl reached the caliche beyond it. Caliche Fluid Systems, the solids-control contractor, recovered the mud by vacuum truck and moved the stained soil into lined bins for disposal.

As our spill procedure requires for any release over 5 bbl, IR-26-0417 was reported by phone at 07:50 to the RRC. At 13:30 an inspector arrived from the district office. INS-26-0331, conducted by the RRC, followed up IR-26-0417. INS-26-0331 inspected mud pump 2, and it also covered the repaired line and the cleanup. The inspector, Teresa Villanueva, cited no violation but asked for the soil sample results within 30 days.

### IR-26-0418, kick in the Wolfcamp B

IR-26-0418, logged at 02:40 while drilling the curve at 9,742 ft MD, occurred in the Hollis Ranch 31-18 2H well. The driller saw a 14 bbl gain and a positive flow check. He shut the well in on the annular preventer of the BOP stack at 02:46, with 180 psi on the drill pipe and 310 psi on the casing. The crew weighted up to 10.6 ppg and circulated the gas out through the choke manifold and the mud-gas separator. IR-26-0418 was not reported to the RRC, because the influx stayed inside the well and nothing was released. An early text to drilling engineering said that IR-26-0418 involved mud pump 1; that was wrong, and both pumps ran normally throughout the kill.
