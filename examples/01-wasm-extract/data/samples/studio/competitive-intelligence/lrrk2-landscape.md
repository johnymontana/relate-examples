# LRRK2 inhibitors in Parkinson's disease: competitive landscape

**From:** Competitive Intelligence, Saltire Biologics · **To:** Neuroscience portfolio committee · **Date:** 18 November 2026 · **Ref:** CI-NEU-2026-14

## Summary

LRRK2 is the most contested kinase target in Parkinson's disease. Thirteen oral inhibitors from eleven companies have entered the clinic since 2013: eight are still active, five have been discontinued, and three have registered a Phase 3. Our own asset, SBA-3857, is in Phase 1 and at least two years behind the leaders. The patent estate sits with three small companies, none of which has a LRRK2 asset in Phase 3, and two of them are valued below $400 million. We recommend opening talks with Ulverston Bio, with Ortolan Bio as the fallback.

## The leaders

Merrowby Pharma is furthest ahead. MPE-7450 is being tested in NCT99358788, a Phase 3 study in Parkinson's disease that has enrolled 2,773 patients since March 2024 and is no longer recruiting; we do not expect a readout before 2028. MPE-7450 is also the best-characterised molecule in the class. Its Phase 2, NCT99357487, enrolled 411 patients from November 2022.

Larkfield Pharma is the surprise. LPA-3882 completed a 317-patient Phase 2, NCT99105093, and moved into NCT99106394, a Phase 3 that has been recruiting since March 2024 and aims to enrol 1,081 patients. Quillon Biosciences has finished the Phase 2 of QBU-6665, NCT99170143, in 387 patients, and expects NCT99171444, a 752-patient Phase 3, to start in June 2027.

Among the Phase 2 programmes, Ulverston Bio's UBL-5457 is the one we would buy. It completed a 31-patient Phase 1, NCT99193561, which started in September 2022. NCT99194862, its 171-patient Phase 2, has been recruiting since February 2024. Davenholt Pharma took DPA-9704 into NCT99266417, a Phase 2 in Parkinson's disease, in February 2025, three months after its Phase 1 began.

## What has failed

KME-058, APS-1223, MPE-6073, LBO-1000 and PPE-5278, all LRRK2 inhibitors for Parkinson's disease, have been discontinued, each after a trial that was terminated early.

| APS-1223 | Ashcombe Pharma | NCT99246902 | Phase 2 | May 2024 | Safety: adverse events above the prespecified threshold |
| MPE-6073 | Merrowby Pharma | NCT99375701 | Phase 2 | Nov 2021 | Futility boundary crossed at the interim analysis |
| LBO-1000 | Lowther Biosciences | NCT99344477 | Phase 2 | Jul 2018 | Sponsor decision: portfolio reprioritisation |
| PPE-5278 | Pendleford Pharma | NCT99417333 | Phase 2 | Jun 2014 | Sponsor decision: portfolio reprioritisation |

The two safety stops are the ones that matter. KME-058 and APS-1223 were both halted when adverse events exceeded the prespecified threshold, and they are the two most recent failures in the class. Merrowby Pharma also developed MPE-6073, which crossed its futility boundary at the interim analysis; that Merrowby kept MPE-7450 going suggests the problem was the molecule rather than the target. The terminations of PPE-5278 in 2014 and LBO-1000 in 2018 were portfolio decisions and tell us little about LRRK2.

All six carry the same title, so the claims will have to be read one by one before any deal. Ulverston Bio's earliest filing, US19143321B2, has expired, but its two granted patents run into the late 2030s. US19356157B2 and EP9055721B1 are assigned to Kestrel Molecular, which has no live programme but is still prosecuting the European filing. Merrowby Pharma, Larkfield Pharma and Quillon Biosciences hold no LRRK2 filing in our set, so their freedom to operate rests on patents we have not found or on licences we cannot see.

## Acquisition screen

Three companies valued below $2 billion have a live LRRK2 programme, and a fourth holds LRRK2 patents without one.

- Ulverston Bio, valued at $1.84 billion, is the best fit: UBL-5457 is in Phase 2, two of its LRRK2 patents are granted, and it has a second Parkinson's programme in late development. That programme is UBL-4373, a GBA1 activator for Parkinson's disease, which has been in NCT99198765, a 359-patient Phase 3, since July 2022.
- Larkfield Pharma, at $905 million, has the only Phase 3 LRRK2 asset at a small company but no LRRK2 patent in our set, so the exclusivity of LPA-3882 is the first diligence question.
- Ortolan Bio, at $287 million, is the cheapest. OBR-301 has been in Phase 1 since April 2025, and Ortolan's granted patent, US19241553B2, covers the same chemistry as Ulverston's.
- Kestrel Molecular, at $395 million, has had no clinical programme since KME-058 was discontinued, but its two patents could be bought on their own.

## What we could not confirm

- A trade report says Ashcombe Pharma has in-licensed OBR-301 for North America; Ortolan Bio has not confirmed it.
- OBR-301 may also inhibit GBA1, according to one conference abstract, although Ortolan describes it as selective for LRRK2.
- US19241553B2 is assigned to Ortolan Bio, not to Larkfield Pharma, as one patent database records it.
- EP9055721B1 may be abandoned before grant if Kestrel Molecular finds no buyer, according to a former employee.
- An earlier edition of this brief listed NCT99375701 as a Phase 3 study; it was a Phase 2, and the table above is correct.

## Recommendation

1. Open talks with Ulverston Bio this quarter, before UBL-5457 reads out and the price moves.
2. Keep Ortolan Bio as the fallback. If that deal goes ahead, Saltire Biologics would own US19241553B2 outright.
3. Commission a freedom-to-operate review of SBA-3857 against US19356157B2 and EP9055721B1.
4. Track NCT99358788 and NCT99106394: a positive readout from either would raise every price on this list.
