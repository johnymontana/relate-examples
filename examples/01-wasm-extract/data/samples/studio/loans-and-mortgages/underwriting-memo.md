# Re-underwriting memo QC-2026-031: L-00712 and five other second liens in early payment default

**To:** Chief Credit Officer; Second-Lien Credit Committee · **From:** Quality Control Underwriting, Lakeshore Mortgage Company · **Date:** October 14, 2026 · **Reference:** QC-2026-031 · **Data as of:** September 30, 2026

## Summary

L-00712, a $132,400 fixed-rate home equity loan to Brandon Wagner, closed on October 8, 2025 and is now in default: nine of its ten scheduled installments are unpaid. Our early payment default policy requires the file to be underwritten again from the documents, and this memo records the result. L-00712 is not alone. Between September and December 2025 we made six second liens that Meridian Servicing Group now reports in default, each behind a first mortgage on a house re-appraised 33 to 54 percent higher within eight months of its purchase. Loan officers Carolyn Jensen and Adam Schmidt originated three each. Each file met our second-lien guidelines on its own; together they point to inflated valuations.

## The file: L-00712

Brandon Wagner submitted application A-00716 on September 7, 2025, for a fixed-rate home equity loan of $139,100 to renovate his primary residence. Application A-00716 was handled by loan officer Carolyn Jensen (NMLS 1789295), who works for Lakeshore Mortgage Company, and resulted in L-00712. L-00712 was made by Lakeshore Mortgage Company at 7.875 percent over 15 years and closed on October 8. L-00712 is serviced by Meridian Servicing Group.

The file also held his purchase application of five months earlier, and the two do not agree:

| Application | Submitted | Credit score | Gross monthly income | Debt-to-income | Value relied on |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Application A-00715, the purchase | Apr 5, 2025 | 722 | $9,750 | 22.4% | $272,000 (AP-00550) |
| Application A-00716, this loan | Sep 7, 2025 | 663 | $4,200 | 42.8% | $420,000 (AP-00551) |

Neither the fall in income nor the 59-point fall in the credit score is explained anywhere in application A-00716.

## The collateral: 4594 Lexington Blvd, Indianapolis

L-00712 is secured, in subordinate lien position, on 4594 Lexington Blvd. The property (PR-0404) is a site-built single-family house in Indianapolis, IN 46207. The first lien on 4594 Lexington Blvd is L-00711, a $238,600 mortgage that Prairie State Credit Union made to Brandon Wagner on May 8, 2025, for the purchase. Application A-00715, his purchase application for a 30-year fixed-rate mortgage, was handled by loan officer Timothy Collins of Prairie State Credit Union and resulted in L-00711. Prairie State services L-00711 in house, and the loan is current.

AP-00550 put the value of 4594 Lexington Blvd at $272,000 on April 7, 2025, for the purchase. AP-00551, ordered for application A-00716, put the value at $420,000 on September 20, 2025. That is a rise of 54.4 percent in five months, with no permits, invoices or photographs of improvements in the file. Carolyn Jensen relied on AP-00551 alone. On the servicers' September 30, 2026 balances the two liens total $367,330, a combined loan-to-value of 87.5 percent on the later appraisal and 135.0 percent on the earlier one. A field review of AP-00551, due in November, is expected to come in closer to $290,000.

## Recommendations

1. Suspend the second-lien authority of Carolyn Jensen and Adam Schmidt while their 2025 files are reviewed.
2. Order field reviews of all six later appraisals, starting with AP-00551.
3. Value any second lien on the lower of the last two appraisals when they are less than twelve months apart.
4. Put L-00738 on the watch list and ask Michael Lee for an up-to-date financial statement.

If the committee approves, L-00712 and the other five loans will be serviced by Lakeshore Mortgage Company's own default unit from January 2027.
