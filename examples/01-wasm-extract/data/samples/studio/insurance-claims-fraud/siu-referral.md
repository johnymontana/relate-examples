# Special Investigations Unit referral SIU-2025-0412

**To:** Head of Claims Fraud · **From:** SIU desk, Birmingham · **Date:** 14 October 2025 · **Status:** referred for recorded interviews

## Summary

One vehicle, VIN ZNU67YFGJLPVYMW6G, is named on six personal-injury claims lodged by six different claimants between January 2024 and September 2025. Ryan Roberts owns ZNU67YFGJLPVYMW6G. Between them the six claims seek £220,744. Every one rests on a medical assessment by one of three practitioners who are, together, attached to 600 of the 5,000 claims in the motor portfolio. Taken one at a time, each claim passed first-notification triage without a flag. Taken together, they look like a staged-accident ring, and we recommend suspending payment on all six pending recorded interviews.

## The vehicle

ZNU67YFGJLPVYMW6G is a grey Vauxhall Astra estate kept at an address in Leicester. Ryan Roberts bought it in 2022 and is still its registered keeper. ZNU67YFGJLPVYMW6G was involved in CL-000761 on 9 January 2024, the first claim in the cluster, when it was said to have pulled out of a side road on the A47 and been struck by the claimant's car. It was named again in the five claims that followed: ZNU67YFGJLPVYMW6G was involved in CL-004122, CL-003544, CL-000553, CL-004177 and CL-000587. Two of those, CL-000553 and CL-004177, describe separate collisions on the same afternoon, 30 April 2025, twelve miles apart.

Ryan Roberts also keeps two other cars. One of them, 529G247Y595BTS56E, was declared a total loss after a fire in 2023. He bought GZFCRDJ8M1RSD5C09 at auction in May 2025. Neither of those vehicles appears on any claim in this cluster.

CL-003544, the largest claim in the cluster at £51,201, was filed by Ryan Roberts himself: he claimed as a passenger in a friend's car that, by his account, was hit by his own Astra on 19 September 2024 while it was being driven by someone he had lent it to. Margaret Wright, the claimant on CL-000587, lives two streets from Ryan Roberts in Leicester. The claim forms for CL-000553 and CL-004177 were submitted from the same IP address within an hour of each other.

## The medical evidence

CL-000761 was assessed by Dr. Eric Robinson, who diagnosed a whiplash-associated disorder and prescribed twelve weeks of physiotherapy. Dr. Robinson assessed CL-004122 as well, eight weeks later, with an almost identical report. CL-000587, a claim for £56,685, was assessed by Dr. Eric Robinson too. Dr. Jason Brown, a physiotherapist practising in Solihull, signed the assessments behind CL-003544 and CL-004177. CL-000553 was assessed by Dr. Ruth Butler, a general practitioner in Coventry.

Across the portfolio, Dr. Ruth Butler, Dr. Jason Brown and Dr. Eric Robinson are each attached to 200 claims; the median practitioner on our panel is attached to six. Their links to the claimants also run outside the claims. Dr. Eric Robinson has treated Margaret Wright and Carl Ward at his Leicester clinic since 2023. Dr. Jason Brown treats Aaron Richardson and Kyle Cook privately, outside any claim. Dr. Ruth Butler treats Carl Ward. A practitioner who treats a claimant privately and then assesses that claimant's injury for us is a conflict our panel terms forbid, whatever the merits of the injury.

## The claimants

CL-000011 was assessed by Dr. Jason Brown. Carl Ward owns six vehicles, among them RB5HMNVRH9UBTPJJK, a panel van insured on a separate commercial policy; RB5HMNVRH9UBTPJJK was involved in CL-000735. GJXWBTWXGM9EX96F7 is registered to Margaret Wright. Kyle Cook and Aaron Richardson have each filed two claims, both lodged on 30 April 2025; neither owns a vehicle on our records. Karen Gonzalez owns 7CK4SZMKG152C1WLS, a hatchback she has insured with us since 2021.

## What we could not establish

Several points in the file do not hold up, and we have left them out of the pattern above:

- Dr. Ruth Butler did not assess CL-000587; the report bearing her name on that file belongs to a different claim and was misfiled.
- Ryan Roberts may also own GJXWBTWXGM9EX96F7, but the DVLA record we hold is two years old and unclear.
- Karen Gonzalez alleges that Dr. Jason Brown treated her after the January collision; his practice has no record of her.
- Aaron Richardson is expected to file a further claim, CL-004903, for about £30,000 once his physiotherapy ends.
- An earlier note that Kyle Cook owns ZNU67YFGJLPVYMW6G was wrong and has been corrected: he was a passenger.

## Recommendation

Suspend payment on CL-000761, CL-004122, CL-003544, CL-000553, CL-004177 and CL-000587, and invite all six claimants to recorded interviews. Ask the three practitioners for their appointment diaries for the relevant dates. Refer the vehicle's history to the Insurance Fraud Bureau once the interviews are complete, and remove Dr. Eric Robinson, Dr. Jason Brown and Dr. Ruth Butler from the assessment panel while the review is open.
