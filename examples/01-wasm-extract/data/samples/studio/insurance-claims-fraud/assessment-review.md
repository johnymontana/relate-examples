# Medical assessment review MAR-2025-0087: three panel practitioners

**To:** SIU desk, Birmingham · **From:** Claims Medical Unit · **Date:** 4 November 2025 · **Reference:** SIU-2025-0412

## Scope

After the referral of 14 October, the SIU asked for a review of the reports signed by Dr. Eric Robinson, Dr. Jason Brown and Dr. Ruth Butler. I read every assessment each of them wrote on a motor claim with an incident in 2024 or 2025, and compared it with the claimant's GP record where we hold one. This review covers the medical evidence only. Liability and the vehicle history stay with the SIU.

## Dr. Eric Robinson

Dr. Eric Robinson, a general practitioner with a private clinic in Leicester, assessed CL-000761, CL-004122 and CL-000587, three of the six claims in the referral. The three reports are near copies: each records a grade II whiplash-associated disorder, the same loss of neck rotation to the degree, and twelve weeks of physiotherapy. Margaret Wright filed CL-000587 for £56,685, and the report on it cites an X-ray dated two days before the collision.

Ryan Roberts filed CL-000306 after a collision on 28 August 2025, seeking £50,923, and Dr. Robinson assessed that claim too. Dr. Eric Robinson treats Ryan Roberts at his Leicester clinic and has done since 2024; Carl Ward and Margaret Wright are also his private patients.

## Dr. Jason Brown

CL-003544 and CL-004177 were both assessed by Dr. Jason Brown, a physiotherapist practising in Solihull. Kyle Cook, the claimant on CL-004177, filed CL-000409 in July 2025, and Dr. Brown assessed that claim as well. CL-002171, a claim by Aaron Richardson for £46,439, was assessed by Dr. Brown too. Dr. Jason Brown treats Kyle Cook and Aaron Richardson privately at the same Solihull clinic where he examined them for us. As a physiotherapist he may assess soft-tissue injuries only, yet two of his reports diagnose a fractured rib.

## Dr. Ruth Butler

Dr. Ruth Butler, a general practitioner in Coventry, assessed CL-000553 and later CL-000983 and CL-001991. Carl Ward filed CL-000983 after a collision on 15 January 2025. CL-001991, a claim for £54,472, was filed by Ryan Roberts. Dr. Ruth Butler treats Carl Ward as his registered GP and has done since 2019, and Ryan Roberts since 2021. Her reports are the most thorough of the three, but CL-001991 is expected to rise to about £60,000 once Dr. Butler's final prognosis is in.

## Points we could not settle

- Dr. Ruth Butler has never treated Kyle Cook; his GP is in Solihull.
- Margaret Wright may also be a patient of Dr. Ruth Butler, but the practice has not yet answered our request.
- Aaron Richardson alleges that Dr. Eric Robinson examined him for CL-002171; the only report on the file is Dr. Brown's.
- An earlier panel note naming Dr. Jason Brown as the assessor on CL-001186 was a keying error and has been withdrawn.

## Recommendations

1. Keep all three practitioners off the assessment panel until the SIU review closes.
2. Have the six claims in the table re-examined by an independent consultant in trauma and orthopaedics.
3. Add those six claims to SIU-2025-0412, since every one was assessed by a practitioner who also treats the claimant, which our panel terms forbid.
