# Spatial Analysis: Mill Creek Flood Zones

Nadia Ferrante works for the Larkspur GIS Office. The Larkspur GIS Office is located at Iowa City. Nadia Ferrante participated in the Spring Flood Mapathon.

## Layers

The Flood Atlas project includes the Hydrology layer. The Flood Atlas project includes the Flood Zones layer. The Hydrology layer contains the Mill Creek channel. The Hydrology layer contains the Bracken Dam spillway. The Flood Zones layer contains the Mill Creek floodplain.

## Survey

The Mill Creek channel was surveyed by the spring field survey. The Bracken Dam spillway was surveyed by the spring field survey. The Mill Creek floodplain was surveyed by the spring field survey. The Bracken Dam spillway was not surveyed by the autumn field survey.
